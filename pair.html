Connect this iPad to your PC

PadLink Go controls your classroom PC and prepares boards anywhere, even offline. To connect:

  1. Tap Share (the square with the arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Open PadLink Go from your Home Screen and tap Connect to your PC.
  3. Scan the QR code on the PC screen again, then click Allow on the PC.

Already have PadLink Go? Just open it and tap Connect to your PC.

Other ways

Can’t scan in PadLink Go? Copied ✓ then in PadLink Go: Connect to your PC → Have a pairing link instead? → Paste.

Just control the PC, without the app: Use the PadLink page in Safari.
