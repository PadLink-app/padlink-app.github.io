Whiteboard tips

The tools

On the left. Pick a color and size in the panel beside them; click the active tool again to tuck the panel away. A board opens with the Pen on and its panel closed, so the whole page is in view: tap the Pen for its colors and sizes.

Pen and highlighter

The pen follows Pencil pressure. Pen types: pen, marker, colored pencil, crayon and chalk. The highlighter sits under the ink, so writing stays readable.

Eraser

Click it again for its modes: Partial rubs out just the part you go over, Whole stroke removes a whole line, Object removes anything you touch (shapes, text, notes, pictures too). The Pencil's Eraser on the iPad works too.

Broom and Hand

The broom (top bar) clears everything you added to this page: drawings, shapes, text, notes and pictures. Locked things and the page's background (an imported or captured page) stay, so a prepared page is ready again for the next class. Hand: drag to move around the page without drawing, or hold Space with any tool.

Laser

Point without drawing: a dot, a fading trail, a light saber or a spotlight, in 8 colors. Students see it too while they follow your board, or while a board is on show.

Shapes

One button on the rail: lines, arrows, number lines, axes, rectangles, ellipses, triangles, polygons (3 to 12 sides), fraction circles and bars.

Straight and square

Hold Shift (Keys on the iPad) for straight angles, squares and circles.

Fractions

Set Parts, draw a fraction circle or bar, then click a section with the Shapes tool to shade it. Number lines can count in whole numbers or fractions.

Text (T)

Click the board and type. Select words for bold, italic, underline, text color, highlighting, font and size. The paragraph menu has left, center and right alignment, lists, indent and outdent. On an iPad, PadLink's controls stay below the text so the iPadOS Copy/Paste menu does not cover them. Tab indents a line; Shift+Tab moves it back. While typing, drag the handle on either side of the box to make it wider or narrower: the words wrap to fit (a long web address breaks at the edge). Click a text box again with Text, or double-click (double-tap) it with Select, to change it. On a student's board, words start at size 32 (48 on yours, to read from the back of the room).

Links

In a text box, tap 🔗: type the Words to show (or select them first) and paste the Web address (a Google Doc, Slides, a video). Put the caret in a link and tap 🔗 to change its words or address. Or paste an address straight onto the board, or into a text box. With Select, click (tap) a link to open it: on this PC in your browser, on students' Chromebooks in a new tab. Links travel with Send page, team boards and what you write on students' boards (in Follow me they show, but open only from the student's own board). Only you can add links: students just open them.

Sticky notes (N)

Pick a color, click the board, and type. Click a note again to add to it; a blank one stays. Select moves, resizes and recolors it (Fill).

Math (M)

A math box with its own keyboard: fractions, powers, roots, ÷ × ≤ ≥ and more. In a text box, √x puts math inside the line.

Select (V)

Click anything to select it, and drag to move it. Click inside an empty shape (a circle, a rectangle) to select it too. Drag a corner to resize proportionally, an edge handle to change only its width or height (text: the side handles set how wide it is), or the round knob to rotate. Double-click (or double-tap) a text box to type in it.

Change it

The bar beside the selection changes border color, fill, thickness, and can duplicate, bring to front, send to back or delete. It stays on the screen even when the selection is at the edge. Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V copy and paste selected text, pictures, shapes, strokes or groups; Duplicate still works as before.

Several at once

Drag a box around things, or Ctrl+click. Ctrl+G groups them, Ctrl+Shift+G ungroups.

Lock

🔒 on the bar (Ctrl+L) locks what's selected: it can't be moved, changed or erased, and the broom leaves it. Lock what you prepared before class; the broom then clears only what was added in the lesson. Click a locked thing and choose Unlock to change it. To lock a whole page: Ctrl+A, then 🔒.

Capture

The PC screen behind the board, any open window, or a whole web page, from the Capture button at the top.

Import

PDFs (pick pages, or type ranges like 1-3, 5) and pictures.

Google Drive

Opens Google's file picker in your browser. Docs, Slides and PDFs come in as pages you pick. In PadLink Go: Add → Google Drive (needs the internet, not the PC).

Paste

Ctrl+V: a copied PadLink object keeps its formatting, a copied picture goes on the page, and copied words become a text box.

All your pages

Click the page number to see every page as a picture. Click one to go there; drag one to move it. A page's 👁 hides it from students.

Long pages

Write near the bottom and the page grows taller. Scroll with the mouse wheel or ↑ ↓.

Zoom

− and + at the bottom, Ctrl + mouse wheel (a pinch on a touchpad), Ctrl + / Ctrl −, or pinch on the iPad. On a touch screen (an interactive panel, a touch Chromebook), two fingers move the page and pinch to zoom; one finger draws. Tap the % between them, or press Ctrl+0, to see the whole page. These zoom the board only, never the whole screen, so the tools stay in reach. If the browser's own zoom changed anyway on a Chromebook, the board says how to put it back (Ctrl+0).

Clearing and backgrounds

The broom (top bar) clears drawings first, then pictures; hold it to clear both. With something selected it becomes a trash can that deletes just that. Its box has Also clear locked things and the imported page to clear everything. Undo brings it all back. Plain, grid, lined, dotted or chalkboard: Pages → Paper.

Saves by itself

Every change is saved on this PC a few seconds after you stop, and at once when you switch boards or close PadLink. Each time PadLink starts, you get a fresh board. Boards → Open the boards folder shows where they are; Save as a file keeps a copy anywhere you like.

Open an older board

Click the board's name (top left) to rename it, start a new one, or open or delete older ones.

Save a copy

In the same menu: keeps a second copy, so you can change one and keep the original.

A board as one file

Save as a file makes one .padlink file with every page and picture: keep it anywhere, email it. Double-click one (or Open a board file) to open it as a new board.

Save as a picture

The picture button (top bar) saves the whole page, however tall.

Prepared on the iPad?

Boards from PadLink Go arrive in this list. See PadLink Go in these tips.

One app for the iPad

PadLink Go controls this PC from your iPad or iPhone and prepares lessons anywhere, even offline. Open padlink-app.github.io in Safari (or scan the code in PadLink's window) and tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Pencil and fingers

On an iPad, the Pencil uses the chosen board tool; drag with a finger to move the page and pinch to zoom. With Select, a finger on what's selected moves, resizes or turns it. PadLink Go uses the same Fingers setting as its Control the PC screen. Phones default to using a finger like the Pencil.

Connect once

With Remote mode on, the QR code in PadLink's window (or Boards → Connect PadLink Go here) connects it: in PadLink Go, tap Connect to your PC, scan the code, and click Allow here.

Send

In PadLink Go, tap Send to PC. The board appears in Boards here, with a message. Sending again updates it; if you'd changed it here too, it arrives as a copy. Just opening it or turning its pages here isn't a change.

Needs Remote mode

PadLink Go reaches this PC through Remote mode, from anywhere. Open on the PC sends a board and opens it here; From the PC keeps a copy of one on the iPad. Forget a device in the same window.

Students' own boards

Class (top bar) → Students: open a class, then Show join code. Students scan it on their Chromebooks, pick their name, and you let them in. No app or account.

You lead, or their own pace

Students start with a copy of your lesson pages (or empty boards, if you choose: then Class → Students → Send all my lesson pages gives them your pages later). 👣 You lead: they watch your page live until you click ✏️ Let them draw, then write on their own copy (what you write next shows up there too); turning the page switches drawing off again. 🧭 Their own pace: they move through the pages themselves and answer each page's poll as they get there; Class → Poll shows how far each has got.

Compare boards

In See their boards, click Compare, pick 2 to 4 boards, then Show side by side: they appear here as A, B, C and D, names hidden unless you tick Show names. Stop comparing at the top ends it.

Eyes up

👀 Eyes up (Class → Students) covers every student's screen with Eyes on the teacher until you click it again. Their work stays underneath.

Hide a page

In Pages, click a page's 👁 to hide it from students (an answer key, your notes). It stays on your board. Previous and Next skip hidden pages, so the class never lands on one by accident; an eye tab beside the page bar shows there's one just before or after, and clicking it (or the page in Pages) opens it. On a hidden page, a note at the top says so. With a blank board, Send my page to everyone adds the page you're on to every student's board.

See their boards

Everyone's board at once. Open one to write on it, or Show to class (names hidden unless you choose). The student is paused while it's on show, and their screen looks like everyone else's. ✏️ Let them draw lets them keep working on it (and pauses them again).

Teams

Fill in the Team and Seat columns of the class list (Class → Names → Edit). Class → Students → Teams: home teams, mixed (keep seats), same seats or random. Show teams puts every table here; each Chromebook shows its team, seat and partners.

Team boards

Teams → Start team boards: one shared board per team, live on every teammate's Chromebook. Students change only their own marks. See their boards shows the team boards first, to look at, write on or show to the class; there, tap a mark to see who made it (and remove it), or a member's name to make it view only for them. End team boards puts everyone back on their own board.

Raise hand

Students tap ✋ on their Chromebook. The corner here shows how many hands are up (or names, or nothing: ⚙ at the top right). Tap it, or Class → Students, to Lower them. Their notes show only on your iPad or iPhone.

Polls

Class → Poll: pick Multiple choice (A–D to start; − and + for A–B up to A–H), True/False, Yes/No, 🔢 Number or ✍️ Short answer, and a time limit, then Start the poll. You can type the Question and pick the Correct answer first (both optional), or put the question on the board or ask it aloud. Students tap an answer, then Lock it in; their screen hides which one, and they can change it until the poll closes. This screen shows only how many answered until you close it; then Show results puts the bars here and on their screens, without names. Tap the Correct answer to mark it, if you didn't before Start; the class sees it only when you tap Show the correct answer (in Class → Poll, or under the results here), in green. PDF report makes a printable summary of every poll of the lesson (what to reteach, each student's score), and Export as CSV a spreadsheet, on this PC, with names or without. When you're done, ✓ Finish the poll takes it off this screen and the students’ screens.

Draw polls

Poll → ✏️ Draw: students draw their answer on a pad, on white or on a picture of this page (Draw on a picture of the whiteboard page), with full screen and zoom. Their drawings show in the Poll tab once the poll is closed (on your iPad, as they come in): pick 2 to 4 and Show side by side, or Show all, names hidden unless you tick Show names. The 📊 Poll editor has ✏️ Draw too.

Number and short-answer polls

🔢 Number: students type a number (1,000 and 1000, or 3/4 and 0.75, count as the same answer). ✍️ Short answer: a word or a few words (capitals and a full stop don't matter). The same answers are grouped into bars. Each student sees only their own answer; this screen shows the answers only once you close the poll (short answers stay folded here until you tap to see them). Tap the correct one to mark it, and Hide any answer you don't want the class to see before Show results. In the 📊 Poll editor you can set the correct answer ahead.

Students during a poll

While a poll is open (not a draw poll), students can't draw: they can zoom in and move the page to see the question (their own view, not yours), and answer. Their tools come back when it closes. Polls in this board → Start keeps your zoom when you're already on that page.

Prepared polls

Page number → 📊 Poll: a poll for this page, with its question, answers, correct answer and time limit. Multiple choice starts with A–D: + Add a choice adds one (up to H), and × beside a choice takes it away (the rest move up a letter). + Add question puts several on one page (a worksheet): in the lesson tap Start question 1 of 4, and after each one Next: question 2 of 4 (it waits for your tap). 📋 Paste questions turns a list into pages, each with its poll, or puts them all on this page: the question, then each answer on its own line, * before the correct one, a blank line between questions; or = 12 (a number) or = Paris (a short answer) under a question for a typed answer. Shuffle puts the buttons in a different order on each Chromebook; A is still A. Both are also in Class → Poll. A page with a poll shows 📊 on its page number, and a tag at the top: Poll ready (or 3 polls ready on this page) before class, Start once a class is open. While the poll is open, its question and answers show across the top of this screen; tap it to make it small.

Lesson so far

Class → Poll lists the board's polls (tap one to go to its page) and sums up the lesson's polls as you go: percent correct, what to reteach, each student's score (folded away here). Show the class puts every poll's bars on this screen without names; tick Show correct answers when you're ready.

At the end

Class settings → Students can use switches tools off for the lesson. Close the class deletes every student board from the PC.
