Help & tips

Pick a topic. The whole PadLink guide, with pictures, is on getpadlink.com.

Connection: green when connected, with the delay to the PC. Tap it for details.

Pen: write, click and drag on the PC. In drawing apps it's a real pen with pressure.

Eraser: the Pencil erases in drawing apps and on the whiteboard.

Right-click: Pencil taps become right-clicks, to open menus.

Laser: point at things on the PC screen without clicking. Tap it again to pick its look and color.

Undo / Redo: Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y on the PC.

Keys: type on the PC with this keyboard, hold Shift, Ctrl or Alt, and shortcuts like Copy and Paste.

PC screen: show the PC's screen here (blue = on). You control the PC either way.

Whiteboard: opens or closes the whiteboard on the PC. The toolbar then shows the whiteboard's own tools.

Class tools: a timer and a name picker, shown big on the PC screen.

Settings: choices for this device, and this help.

Hide toolbar: more room. Tap the small tab at the top to bring it back.

Apple Pencil

  • Each spot here matches the same spot on the PC screen, like a drawing tablet.
  • A light touch moves the cursor; a firm press clicks or drags. Change how firm in Settings.
  • In drawing apps and on the whiteboard it's a real pen, with pressure and tilt.

Fingers

  • Tap to click. Drag to scroll.
  • Pinch to zoom: on the whiteboard, and in Chrome, Word, PowerPoint and PDFs.
  • Long-press or two-finger tap to right-click.
  • Fingers are ignored while you use the Pencil, so a resting hand doesn't click.

On an iPhone

  • One finger acts like the Pencil: it moves, clicks and draws. Two fingers scroll and zoom. Change it in Settings → Fingers.
  • With the PC screen showing, a pinch zooms this view so small text is readable. Whole screen zooms back out; the PC zoom button makes a pinch zoom the PC app instead.

The tools

  • While the whiteboard is open, the toolbar shows its own tools: Select, Pen, Highlighter, Shapes, Eraser, Text, Math, colors and sizes. They stay in step with the PC.
  • The Pencil draws with whatever tool you picked.

Options

  • Tap the tool that's on again for its options: pen types, colors (the rainbow circle is any color), sizes, and Parts for fraction shapes and number lines.

Just the page

  • The iPad shows only the whiteboard's page, filling the screen. To see the whole PC screen, turn off Settings → Whiteboard: show just the page.

On an iPhone

  • Pen, Highlight, Shapes and Eraser are at the bottom. More has the rest: Select, Text, Math, Add, Laser, Keys, Class tools, Boards, Settings and Close the whiteboard.

Laser pointer

  • iPad: under the Eraser. iPhone: in More. It points without drawing; tap it again for its look and color. Any drawing tool turns it off. Students see it too while they follow your board, or while a board is on show.

Gestures

  • Two-finger tap: Undo. Three-finger tap: Redo. Two-finger double-tap: the whole page.

Pages

  • Tap the page number (like “2 / 7”) to see every page as a picture. Tap one to go there.
  • To move a page, hold it, then drag it to its new place.
  • The same menu adds, duplicates and deletes pages.

Long pages and zoom

  • Pages grow taller when you write near the bottom. Drag with two fingers to scroll.
  • Pinch to zoom in; tap % to see the whole page again.

Clearing a page

  • The trash clears the drawings first; tap it again to clear the pictures too. Undo brings them back.

Boards

  • Boards save themselves on the PC (in PadLink's own folder: Boards → Open the boards folder) a few seconds after you stop, and at once when you switch boards or close PadLink. Each time PadLink starts, you get a fresh board.
  • To open an older board or start a new one: More → Boards, or the board name at the top left on the PC.
  • On the PC, Save a copy keeps a second copy of the board, and Save as a file makes one .padlink file to keep anywhere or email. Double-click one to open it.
  • Prepare boards on this iPad without the PC: PadLink Go (Settings → PadLink Go).

Select something

  • Pick Select, then tap a drawing, shape, text or picture. Drag it to move it.

Change it

  • Drag a corner to resize, or the round knob to rotate.
  • The Select panel changes its color, fill and thickness, and can duplicate, bring to front, send to back or delete it.

Several at once

  • Tap Select again for its panel: turn on Add to selection and tap more things, or tap Select all.
  • Group keeps them together; Ungroup splits them again.
  • On the PC: drag a box around things, or Ctrl+click.

Add words

  • Pick Text (iPhone: in More) and tap where the words go.
  • Type, or write with the Pencil (Scribble turns it into text). The words appear on the PC as you go.

Style

  • Select words for bold, italic, underline, color, font and size, or make a list.

Math

  • √x opens the math keyboard: fractions, powers, roots, ÷ × ≤ ≥ and more. ABC brings the normal keyboard back.
  • Done finishes. To change it later, tap it with Text.

Sticky notes

  • Pick Note (iPhone: in More), choose its color, and tap the board: a note appears, ready to type on.
  • Tap a note again with Note or Text to type more. A blank note stays, ready to fill in later.
  • With Select, move it, resize it, or change its color with Fill.

Timer

  • Tap a preset to start, or type minutes and seconds. +0:30 adds time.
  • It counts down in a box on the PC screen, over any app. Pick its size: small, medium or large.
  • When time is up, the PC chimes and the box flashes. Sounds can be turned off.

Name picker

  • Add a class: one row per student (＋ Add student, or paste a column of names into a name box), then Pick a name. A wheel or flashing names play on the PC. ✕ at the end of a row removes a student (it asks first).
  • Everyone once, then repeat gives everyone a turn first. Start over resets it.
  • Class lists are saved on the PC: names, and optionally each student's home team and seat.

Prepare boards anywhere

  • PadLink Go is PadLink's app for this iPad or iPhone: it controls this PC like this page does, and prepares lessons anywhere, even with no internet (photos, screenshots and PDF worksheets too).
  • Tap Send to PC or Open on the PC in PadLink Go, and the board is waiting on the PC. From the PC keeps a copy of a PC board on the iPad.

1. Get it (once)

  • Open PadLink Go in Safari.
  • In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Always open it from there: it then works offline and keeps your boards safe.

2. Connect it to this PC (once)

  • In PadLink Go, tap Connect to your PC → Scan the PC’s code, scan the QR code in PadLink’s window on the PC, and click Allow on the PC.
  • PadLink Go reaches the PC through Remote mode, so keep it on. It opens on your boards: tap Control the PC when you're ready (or switch on Open on this PC by itself under Connect to your PC). Boards (top left) goes back to your boards; the PC stays paired.
  • Can’t scan? Copy a pairing link here, then in PadLink Go: Connect to your PC → Have a pairing link instead? → Paste.

3. Send

  • In PadLink Go, tap Send to PC (one board from its ⋯ menu, or all of them at the top). Boards show Sent ✓ once the PC has them.
  • Changed it later? Send again: the PC's copy is updated. If you'd changed it on the PC too, the new one arrives as a copy, so nothing is lost. Just opening it or turning its pages on the PC isn't a change.
  • Boards stay on the device until sent. For a backup, Share as a file (⋯ menu) to Files or Google Drive.

Students on Chromebooks

  • Each student gets their own whiteboard on their Chromebook: no app or account. Open Class tools → Students (here, or on the PC's whiteboard).
  • Open the class (pick a class list), then Show join code: a QR code and code appear on the PC screen. Students scan it, pick their name, and you Let in each one (or Let everyone in).

Teaching with their boards

  • Follow me: their screens show your board, live. Their own boards gives them back.
  • Send my page to everyone: the page you're on becomes a new page on every student's board.
  • See their boards: everyone's board at once. Open one to write on it, or Show to class (names hidden unless you choose). The student is paused while it's on show, and their screen looks like everyone else's, so it stays anonymous. ✏️ Let them draw lets them keep working on it, and untick it to pause them again, for example if they're distracting the class.
  • Students can use: switch off shapes, text, pictures, pages, Raise hand and more for the lesson.

Teams

  • In the class list (Name picker → Edit), fill in each student's home Team and Seat columns, or paste the name, team and seat columns from a spreadsheet into a name box. Optional: names alone still work.
  • Class tools → Students → Teams: Home teams; Mixed (new teammates, everyone keeps their seat, one of each seat per team); Same seats; or Random (tablemates kept apart). Then Make teams. A class list without Team and Seat numbers offers Random only; add the numbers for the other three.
  • Teams of 4; one extra makes a team of 5, two extras two teams of 3, three extras a team of 3. Only students in the class are placed; one who joins late goes to their home team.
  • Each Chromebook shows Team 3 · Seat 2 and its table, with shoulder and face partners. Show teams puts every team on the PC screen.
  • Tap a name, then another to swap them, or a team's ＋ to move them. Send page on a team sends the whiteboard page you're on to that team only.

Team boards

  • Start team boards (under Teams): each team works on one shared whiteboard, live on every teammate's Chromebook. Their own boards wait, unchanged.
  • Each student can change, move or erase only their own marks; teammates' marks (and yours) are locked for them. On a team board students can't add or delete pages, change the background, clear the page or put in pictures.
  • See their boards shows each team's board first: look closer, write on it (every teammate sees it at once) or Show to class. Send page (on a team, or Send my page to everyone) adds the page you're on to the team boards, worksheet and all.
  • In Look closer on a team board: tap a mark to see who made it, and Remove this mark if needed (it goes from everyone's screen). Tap a member's name at the top to make the board view only for them (they watch, but can't draw), and again to let them draw.
  • End team boards: everyone goes back to their own board. The team boards stay in See their boards until you close the class.

Raise hand

  • Students tap ✋ Raise hand on their Chromebook, with a few words if they like. A ✋ count shows on the Class tools button and the Students tab.
  • Class tools → Students lists the hands in the order raised, with how long each has waited and their words (only here, never on the PC screen). Tap Lower, or Lower all.
  • The PC decides when to chime, so it can't be used to make noise: once per student, at most every 15 seconds, and a reminder for a hand up 2 minutes. Switch the chime on or off here and on the PC.
  • The ✋ beside a name switches Raise hand off for that student. The times and what the PC screen shows (how many, names or nothing) are in the PC's ⚙ Settings → Raise hand (or the whiteboard's ⚙).

Polls

  • Class tools → Poll: pick the answers (A–B up to A–E, True/False or Yes/No) and a time limit, then Start the poll. Put the question on the board, or ask it aloud.
  • Students tap an answer, then Lock it in. Their screen then hides which answer they chose, so a neighbour can't copy it. They can change it until the poll closes, and an answer tapped but not locked in still counts.
  • Here you see the answers come in. The whiteboard (the class can see it) shows only how many have answered until you Close the poll (or time runs out).
  • Show results puts the bars on the whiteboard and every Chromebook, without names. Who chose what is only in Class tools. New poll clears it.
  • Once it's closed, tap the Correct answer (optional): it shows in green, and each Chromebook says whether they got it.
  • Prepared polls: on the whiteboard, the page number → 📊 Poll saves a poll with the page (question, answers, correct answer, time limit). On that page, Start this page's poll appears here and on the whiteboard. 📋 Paste questions turns a list of questions into pages with their polls ready.
  • While a poll with a typed question is open, the whiteboard shows the question and its answers across the top, so you type it once. Tap it there to make it small. Switch it off with Show a poll's question on the whiteboard.
  • Shuffle the answers: each Chromebook shows the buttons in its own order, so a neighbour's finger gives nothing away. A is still A.
  • Polls in this board lists every page's poll on the whiteboard, with its page and what's set up (or missing). Tap one to go to its page; during class, Start runs it.
  • Lesson so far sums up the lesson's polls as you go: percent correct, Reteach? and the most chosen wrong answer, and each student's score. Show the class puts the bars on the whiteboard without names; tick Show correct answers when you're ready to reveal them.
  • The lesson's polls stay until you close the class. PDF report makes a printable report on the PC: each poll's bars and percent correct, the most chosen wrong answer, who changed their answer, each student's score, and who might need another look. Export as CSV saves the same answers as a spreadsheet. Both go in Documents › PadLink › Poll results. Untick With students' names for Student 1, 2… instead. Closing the class deletes the polls.

Joining and ending

  • Students join through: the school network (no Remote mode data) or Remote mode, if Chromebooks can't reach the PC. With the school network, a student on another Wi-Fi (their own phone, say) can use Student on another Wi-Fi? on the join code: a second code through Remote mode. They show as Remote mode in the Students list.
  • Close the class at the end: every student board is deleted from the PC.

The first time

  • Scan the QR code in PadLink on the PC. After that this device is remembered.

Each day

  • The first time, this device and the PC show a 4-digit number. Click Allow on the PC if they match.
  • After that it connects by itself for 30 days (change it in the PC's ⚙ Settings → Connecting). Disconnect on the PC means Allow again next time.
  • Only one device controls the PC at a time. Taking over from another iPad or iPhone always asks Allow on the PC.
  • Tip: Share → Add to Home Screen for one-tap PadLink.

School Wi-Fi

  • If the page never loads, turn on Remote mode on the PC and sign in there once.
  • If Microsoft shows a notice page first, tap Continue.
  • Remote mode has 5 GB a month free from Microsoft. PadLink's window on the PC shows how much is used.

Lost a device?

  • On the PC: ⚙ Settings → Connecting → Paired iPads and iPhones… → Forget. Devices unused for 60 days are forgotten by themselves.
