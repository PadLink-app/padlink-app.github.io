PadLink Tracing (padlink-tracing.woff): the dashed print letters, on writing lines, of the Tracing font in text boxes.

Made for PadLink by laying round dots along the single strokes of Hershey Sans 1-stroke
(from https://gitlab.com/oskay/svg-fonts, fonts/Hershey/HersheySans1.svg). The tool that builds it is in
the PadLink repository: tools/make-tracing-font.py.
